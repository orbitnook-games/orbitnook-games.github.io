Privacy Policy – Neon Blocks

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Neon Blocks is a puzzle game published by Orbitnook (the Netherlands). This policy explains what data the game uses. Contact: orbitnook.games@gmail.com.

No account, no personal details from you

You do not need an account. The game does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone or precise location.

Data stored on your device

Your high score, statistics, achievements, daily-challenge progress and settings (sound, vibration, language) are stored locally on your device only. Uninstalling the game removes them.

Analytics (Firebase)

We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the game is used and to improve it (for example how many games are played, how long sessions last and where players stop). This uses an app-instance identifier and technical information such as device model, operating system version, app version, language and approximate region (derived from IP address). We do not use it to identify you personally. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's advertising ID, IP address and technical device data to show ads and measure their performance. Where required (EEA, UK), the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before showing personalised ads, and you can change your choice afterwards in the game's settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads). See how Google uses data in advertising.

In-app purchase

If you buy the option to remove ads, the payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details; we only receive confirmation that the purchase was made.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. Data is only processed by Google (Firebase and AdMob) as described above.

Children

Neon Blocks is not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may request access to, correction or deletion of data about you, or object to processing. Because we cannot identify you from the data we hold, contact us by email and we will help where possible. You also have the right to complain to your national data protection authority (in the Netherlands: Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

Changes

If we change this policy, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
